# Pup Blast Support

Thanks for playing Pup Blast!

**Need help or found a bug?** Email **[support email]**. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

## Common questions

**I bought Remove Ads but still see ads.**
Open the menu (top-left on the home screen) → **Restore purchases**. Optional reward ads, the ones you choose to watch for bonuses, stay available after Remove Ads.

**I got a new phone. Can I keep my progress?**
Progress is saved on your device. If you restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one, your progress comes with it. Remove Ads can always be restored from the menu.

**How do I change my ad privacy choices?**
Menu → **Privacy choices** (shown in regions where ad consent applies). To turn off tracking for all apps, go to iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

**How do I see the leaderboards?**
Tap the trophy on the home screen. You need to be signed in to Game Center (iPhone Settings → Game Center).

**How do digs work?**
Every dig finds a random pup. Rare pups are found 27% of the time, Epic 9% and Legendary 2%. You are guaranteed an Epic or better at least once every 30 digs, and a Legendary at least once every 90. Digs use bones you earn by playing, a free weekly dig, or a free daily dig after a short ad. Digs can never be bought with money.
