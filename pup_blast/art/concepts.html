Pup Art Directions

Bean pups are now the game's art. Sticker sheet is drawn from the same breed data, so either can ship, and dev mode can switch the game between them. Commons and rares are 17 different breeds. Epics and legendaries are dressed-up versions of those breeds.

In the game

Bean pups

Full-body sitting pups with a hand-wobbled colored line. Each breed gets its own ears, snout, markings and tail. Faces mix 10 eye styles (round, puppy, sparkly, happy, wink, almond, sleepy, pug, husky blue, lashes) with dog mouths: an open pant with the tongue out, a wide grin, a small smile, or a pug mouth. Rares wear a bandana in the rare color.

Alternate

Sticker sheet

Head-only stickers with an ink outline and die-cut white border. The Shiba is redrawn: cream cheeks and brows without outlines, thicker ears and a longer snout.

Rarer versions

Same breed, dressed up

The breed stays recognizable, and the rarity shows in what the pup wears: sunglasses, a captain's hat, a tiara, a wizard hat, headphones, then a crown or halo with a glow for legendaries.

What makes them dogs
No cat-style ω mouths: dogs pant with their tongue out or grin wide. Big noses on a wide snout, prick ears that are broad at the base with rounded, fluffy tips, and a dog's tail: curled, plumed, otter or nub.
Before final art
These are drawn by code from about 15 traits per breed. A single illustrator should redraw the final set from this sheet so the line and proportions match.

Mood references: Neko Atsume and its dog follower Boku to Wanko for the soft, collectible feel.
