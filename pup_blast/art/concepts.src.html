Pup Art Directions

The same three pups drawn four ways, on the game's own background. The small icons on the right are buddy-bar size (44px), where most players will actually see them.

Now

Current placeholder

Perfect circles, centered features and an even glow make it read as generic clip art. Everything below breaks at least one of those habits.

Direction A

Sticker sheet

The look
Die-cut stickers: thick ink outline, white border, a slight tilt. Small eyes set low and wide give each face a goofy, specific expression.
Why it feels made
The heavy line and off-center pose read as a hand-picked choice. It also holds up best at 44px.
To make 24+
Easy. Same head shape and new ears and markings, close to the current trait system.
Animation
Wobble, squash and peel-off reveals in the gacha all fit naturally.

Reference mood: LINE-style sticker packs, and the soft kawaii art of Neko Atsume.

Direction B

Bean pups

The look
Full-body sitting loaves with stubby paws and a tail. A colored, slightly wobbly line instead of black ink.
Why it feels made
The jittery line and whole-body poses feel drawn by hand. Breeds read by silhouette and tail, not only by color.
To make 24+
Medium. Every breed needs its own body shape, ears and tail.
Animation
Best of the four: tail wags, hops and sleeping loaves give the most life.

Reference mood: Neko Atsume and its dog follower Boku to Wanko (keep the feel, not the drawings).

Direction C

Riso print

The look
Two or three spot inks that overprint into new colors, with print grain and slightly off-register layers. The dogs are built from simple geometric shapes.
Why it feels made
The deliberate misprints and the limited ink palette are the opposite of a smooth gradient render. It's the most distinctive and the most "indie".
To make 24+
Easy once the shape kit exists. The palette stays fixed, so new pups are always on-brand.
Animation
Limited. It suits a stepped "printing" reveal more than bouncy motion.

Reference mood: Naomi Wilkinson's sight hound riso print, animal riso illustrations.

Direction D

Pixel pups

The look
16×16 sprites scaled up crisp, with a dark outline and a small palette per breed.
Why it feels made
Every pixel is a visible decision, so it never reads as generated. It pairs naturally with the blocky board.
To make 24+
Cheapest to commission. Pixel artists on itch.io sell and take commissions for exactly this.
Animation
Two-to-four-frame idles and barks are cheap and charming.

Reference mood: 32×32 pixel animal pack, itch.io dog sprite assets, SMOLCOFE's Hungry Puppy, CC0 dog sprites.

My pick: A, with B's poses later

Sticker sheet stays readable at buddy-bar size, fits the chunky candy board, and is the cheapest to grow to 24 pups and beyond. Bean pups would make the best gacha reveal, so once a style is locked, full-body art could be added just for the reveal and the Kennel.

Whichever you pick, these are direction sketches, not final assets. The final 24 should come from one illustrator so they stay consistent.
